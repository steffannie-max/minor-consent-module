Trauma Informed Oregon

Involvement Decision Guide

Parent and guardian involvement in outpatient behavioral health care

Use this as a conversation guide. Follow current Oregon law, organizational policy, supervision, and clinical judgment.

A trauma-informed pathway

StepQuestions to consider
1. AskWhat does the young person understand about involvement? Who feels supportive, safe, or unsafe to include?
2. AssessWhat are the potential benefits, risks, and clinical reasons related to involvement?
3. CollaborateWhat form of involvement could work: a shared meeting, part of a session, a collaborative letter, or another agreed approach?
4. ConsultWhat do supervision, peer consultation, and organizational policies clarify?
5. DocumentRecord what was considered, who was consulted, the decision, and the clinical reasoning.
6. RevisitConditions can change. Set a point to review the decision again.

Documentation prompts

  • What did the young person identify as helpful or concerning?
  • What safety and care considerations were assessed?
  • What alternatives were offered?
  • What consultation occurred?
  • When will this decision be reviewed?
