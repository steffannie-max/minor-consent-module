Oregon consent overview

Service typeAge or conditionGeneral point
Medical and dentalAge 15 and olderMay consent to services provided by specified licensed professionals without parent or guardian consent.
Reproductive healthVaries by serviceOregon permits minors to consent to several reproductive and sexual health services. Confirm current OHA guidance for the specific service.
Outpatient behavioral healthAge 14 and olderMay consent to outpatient mental health and substance use treatment under ORS 109.675.
